# V1 — persistent realtime Gizmo

## In scope

- Existing native emulator and the existing Oddity OS body protocol
- Gemini 3.1 Flash Live for realtime voice, camera frames, typed tools, and native Google Search grounding
- `GizmoSession` as the single owner of lifecycle, identity, transcript, memory, tool, error, and reconnect behavior
- 24 kHz device PCM resampled to Gemini's 16 kHz input; 24 kHz model output returned unchanged
- final transcript JSONL for debugging, Memobase, and future analytics
- self-hosted Memobase on Railway behind `MemoryProvider`
- Gemini 3.7 Flash behind `ReasoningProvider` and `deep_think()`
- typed `deep_think()` and a placeholder `set_expression()` bus (not the character; glass architecture comes after Jun's design)

## Explicitly out of scope

- H3 Max and the Adaptive Media director
- image or video generation
- games
- parent dashboard
- a custom AI router
- a second custom semantic-memory system
- unnecessary microservices

There is no offline fallback: without a Gemini key the brain refuses to start, and with one, an outage is reported rather than papered over.

## Milestones

1. Existing emulator ↔ Gemini 3.1 Flash Live with stable realtime voice.
2. Memobase memory reused across separate `GIZMO_USER_ID` sessions.
3. Gemini Live calls `deep_think()` and re-voices the Gemini 3.7 result.
4. Camera frames and native Google Search grounding work cleanly.
